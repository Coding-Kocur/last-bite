    Questions

    Is the food still good?

    Yes. It is food the store made or stocked for today and did not sell: bread from the morning, a meal cooked this evening, groceries close to their date. The date is on every offer, so you decide before you reserve.

    How do I pay?

    By card in the app when you reserve. The store receives the money once you have collected. This website is a preview of the app, so nothing is charged here.

    Where do I collect?

    At the store, during the collection time shown on the offer. We are starting in Nicosia.

    Will the app be in Greek?

    Yes, in Greek and in English.

    The page for stores explains the costs and lets you post an offer to try it.
